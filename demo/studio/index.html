Feet

IK on: the hand / foot follows its box control (keyed), the elbow / knee points at its pole (diamond). Without keys a control follows the animation; keys go to the selected anim layer (BaseAnimation: the rig itself). Moving a control or typing a value in the channel box keys it. IK/FK blend: 1 = IK, 0 = FK; key it (◆) to switch over time, and use Match to snap one onto the other first.

Anim layers

Keys applied over the clips (top of the list last): Additive offsets or Override poses, on the whole timeline or bound to a clip. Anim clips live on the Story timeline: select one to edit it here.

Attribute editor

InJoint⤵Weight

Checked joints are driven by this layer; unchecked joints keep the layers below. Weights between 0 and 1 blend partially. In the viewport, included joints are coloured.
